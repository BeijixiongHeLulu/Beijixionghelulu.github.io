Community evaluations and evidence accumulation

How are community evaluations reflected in decisions?

Second author · Data analysis

Manuscript submitted

Institution
Lifedu Educational Technology Co., Ltd.
Supervision
Dr. Ze Geng, Principal Investigator

I preprocessed electroencephalography (EEG) recordings, extracted centroparietal positivity (CPP) slopes, and fitted and compared behavioral models for this collaborative study.

From recordings to a decision model

I retained valid error responses for decision modeling.

Model fitting and comparison

CPP slopes were extracted from trial-level EEG averaged across Pz, Cz, CP1 and CP2. Models were fitted using aggregate maximum-likelihood estimation in PyDDM and compared using BIC. CPP slope was a measured covariate; drift rate was a model parameter. The model separated accumulation from nondecision time, so boundary-crossing time alone is not the full response time.

Error trials were excluded from response-time and D-score summaries, while valid error choices were retained for decision modeling.
