Preparing to take back control

A VR study of automated-driving takeovers

How do drivers understand a road situation and organize their actions when taking back control?

Independent project · Design, implementation and analysis

Manuscript submitted

Context
VR human-factors experiment

I independently designed the study, extended LoopAR in Unity and C# to run the task and warning sequences, and analyzed driving and physiological records.

Understanding preparation

I separated the opportunity to observe the road from the request to take control. This let me examine what drivers understood before acting and how that preparation related to the braking and steering that followed.

I considered prior driving tendencies alongside drivers’ understanding of the current hazard and physiological changes during preparation. Following braking and steering beyond the first input let me examine how drivers organized the maneuver and how the developing action sequence related to collision risk.
