Preparing to take back control

A VR study of automated-driving takeovers

I studied how drivers prepare to regain control from automation and how their actions unfold after a takeover.

Independent project · Design, implementation and analysis

Manuscript submitted

In this independent project, I designed the experiment, extended LoopAR in Unity and C#, and analyzed behavioral and physiological records.

Preparing to take control

Taking control involves more than a first response. This study separated the opportunity to inspect the road from the actions that followed, asking how prior driving tendencies and preparation related to the transition.

Before the takeover trials, manual-driving observations characterized participants' slowing and steering; questionnaire items about cautious driving provided a separate source of prior information.

To probe what participants had noticed before taking control, I paused the simulated world and hid the road during the questions. Participants answered without further road viewing, while the question timer ran independently of simulation time.

Recording the transition

I implemented event markers and timestamped driving records so task and control transitions could be matched across recordings. Steering, pedal input and speed were recorded alongside event information, and I added an interface for sending markers to physiological acquisition.

Roadworks and pedestrians provided two simulated contexts for the takeover tasks.

Equipment and scenario images from the study materials.

Analyzing action sequences

I examined initial braking and steering, then the sequence of actions after manual control was transferred to the driver. Across the analyses, I related these records to prior-driving observations, responses to the situation-awareness questions and physiological measures, accounting for repeated observations within drivers.

The project shaped my interest in how assistance can support preparation before a demanding action, and how that support may differ across people.
