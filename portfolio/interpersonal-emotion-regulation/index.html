Neural patterns in interpersonal emotion regulation

How do two people coordinate when one helps the other regulate emotion? We compared reappraisal and distraction while recording both people with functional near-infrared spectroscopy (fNIRS).

Co-author · Data analysis

Published 2026

Journal
Emotion

I contributed across the behavioral and fNIRS analyses, including preprocessing, activation analysis, interbrain synchrony and multivariate classification.

Where we measured

What the neural patterns distinguished

Interbrain synchronization patterns distinguished strategy and rating-defined regulation success in within-participant cross-validation. Success meant a more positive rating on the second viewing.

Study and analysis details

After quality checks and preprocessing, activation analyses used task-related oxygenated-hemoglobin estimates. Behavioral and activation models included a dyad random intercept. For interbrain synchrony, trial-level Pearson correlations between ten regions per person formed a 10 × 10 feature matrix. Support-vector machines were evaluated with five-fold within-participant cross-validation.
