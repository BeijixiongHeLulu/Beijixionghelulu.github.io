Human performance during whole-body motion

What shapes directional responses during whole-body motion?

Unity task development · Response-time analysis

Manuscript submitted

Institution
Institute of Software, Chinese Academy of Sciences

I developed a Unity directional-judgment task for an existing six-degree-of-freedom motion platform and analyzed response times in relation to task-relative measured motion.

Responding during motion

Motion relative to the task

Unity controlled task and motion commands; under-seat sensors recorded the motion used in analysis.

Task events linked responses to motion recorded beneath the seat.

How motion entered the response-time analysis

The along-cue component was summarized by its signed mean; the across-cue component by its root mean square. The geometry above explains the reference frame, rather than showing either computed statistic. The pre-cue window did not depend on response time.

Unity emitted cue and response markers. Independently recorded motion was aligned with these task events for analysis; commanded platform settings were not treated as the measured movement.
