Honesty, monitoring and incentives

How do AI monitoring and incentives affect honest reporting?

Co-first author · Experimental design and analysis

Published 2026

Journal
Acta Psychologica Sinica

I helped design the experiments, programmed the PsychoPy tasks, and analyzed trial-level responses with mixed-effects models in Python and R.

A coin-prediction task

Programmed monitoring and feedback were framed as AI oversight.

Monitoring and honest reporting

Honesty was highest when monitoring was visible.

Experiment 2 used uncertain monitoring and followed Experiment 1 in the same 46 participants.

In Experiment 1, the monitoring effect was larger in groups with higher Machiavellianism or psychopathy scores.

After incentives were removed

In the monetary-reward experiment, honesty rose during incentives and remained above baseline after withdrawal. Moral feedback showed no comparable increase.

Separate groups; phases within the same session.

Publication

Wang, J., Jiang, X., Chen, Y., Wang, M., & Du, F. (2026). From Overt Deterrence to Covert Internalization: Moral Effects of AI Regulation and the Moderating Role of Personality Traits. Acta Psychologica Sinica, 58(3), 381–398.
