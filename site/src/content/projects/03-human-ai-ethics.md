This project investigates how AI supervision and incentive mechanisms influence dishonest behavior and ethical decision-making. I designed and programmed the behavioral experiments in PsychoPy and analyzed the data using Python/R and mixed-effects models.

The resulting article was published in _Acta Psychologica Sinica_. [Read the article](https://doi.org/10.3724/SP.J.1041.2026.0381).
