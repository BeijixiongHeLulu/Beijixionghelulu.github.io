title: Human Performance During Whole-Body Motion
summary: Unity experiments and task-relative motion analysis on a 6-DOF platform.
order: 3

---

At the Institute of Software, Chinese Academy of Sciences, I developed a Unity-based directional-judgment task on a 6-DOF motion platform. The study included 39 participants and related response times to trial-level, task-relative components of platform acceleration: directional congruency and lateral interference.

Reaction times showed no clear monotonic gradient across motion-intensity conditions. The two motion components showed opposite associations with response time in the pooled model, but neither association was clearly supported after adjustment for target direction.

Preprint: [arXiv:2601.12884](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.12884).
