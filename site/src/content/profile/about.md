I am a research assistant at Lifedu Educational Technology Co., Ltd., supervised by Dr. Ze Geng (Principal Investigator), and a research intern at the Institute of Software, Chinese Academy of Sciences. I received my B.S. in Applied Psychology from Henan University in 2026.

My research aims to understand people: their cognition, emotions, intentions, internal states, and experiences. I am interested in combining behavioral and eye-tracking data with physiological signals (ECG, skin conductance, and EMG) and neural measures (EEG, MEG, and fNIRS). My goal is to help systems and machines better understand and respond to people, improving efficiency and user experience in human–machine collaboration and human–computer interaction.

I am seeking research-oriented master's programs for **Fall 2027**, with the aim of continuing to a Ph.D.
